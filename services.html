Service · ThermStack

Thermal analysis

Find the limiting hot spot and the margin behind it, on chip, package and board together.
Request this service

Package architecture, power allocation and cooling choices are linked, and hot spots are far smaller than the package they sit on. We keep the whole package in the model and resolve the hot spots on demand.

  • Full-package temperature maps — static and transient, on the real structure, with cross-sections and buried layers.
  • Hot spots resolved on demand — fine resolution where it matters, demonstrated down to 20 µm on a full package.
  • Chip, package and board in one model — see what a chip-only model misses, such as voltage-regulator heating on the board.
  • Workload and cooling studies — compare power and cooling scenarios, with ThermStack-GNN for repeated design-space sweeps.

  • Design files: 3Dblox, GDSII, IPC-2581, ODB++ or ECXML
  • Power maps as CSV, static or time-varying
  • Cooling conditions and your thermal limit
What you get back
  • Interactive 2D/3D temperature views
  • Hot-spot locations and thermal margins
  • Maps and data for your design review
Service · WarpStack

Warpage and stress analysis

See how your package bends and where it is stressed, before assembly does.
Request this service

A few microns of bow can decide whether solder joints open or bridge, and stress concentrates at corners far smaller than the package. We perform warpage and stress analysis for commercial assembly processes, with each question answered at the resolution it needs.

  • Commercial assembly processes — we consider warpage, bump creep, cracks, fatigue and many other effects.
  • Package warpage — warpage maps and peak-to-peak bow, with every layer resolved through the full thickness.
  • Stress at the corners you name — die corners and gaps at the resolution you ask for, demonstrated at 50 µm and 10 µm.
  • Design-space studies — stack-up, material and thermal what-ifs with WarpStack-GNN and WarpStack-MOR.
  • Side-by-side comparison — signed difference maps between any two designs, material sets or resolutions.

What we need from you
  • Stack description in IEEE 3Dblox or floorplan JSON
  • Substrate or board layout in ODB++ or IPC-2581
  • Material properties, or our bundled library
  • The assembly process, thermal load and regions you care about
What you get back

  • Warpage, stress and reliability results
  • CSV and JSON data for your own flow
How it works

From your design files to a decision

Share the design and the question

Your files, your operating conditions, and what you need to decide: peak temperature, warpage limit, or the corners you are worried about.

We build the model

We confirm input compatibility and assumptions with you, then build the model directly from your design files.

We run the analysis

The whole package first, then resolution on demand where the question needs it, with numerical checks on the results that matter.

We review the results with you

You get the maps, the data and a walkthrough of what they mean for your design.
