Capabilities

Signal integrity across the stack, from geometry to margin

Stack architecture, bond and bump pitch, TSV placement and routing all shape how a signal crosses from one die to the next. SIStack brings extraction and channel analysis into one workflow, so these choices can be compared while they are still open.

01 / Extraction

Field solvers in 2D and 3D

Full-wave, quasi-static and PEEC solvers extract the interconnect in 2D cross-sections or full 3D: on-die wires, hybrid bonds, micro-bumps, TSVs, interposer and package traces. Results come out as RLC networks or S-parameters, ready for channel simulation.

02 / AI extraction

Fast geometry-to-RLC extraction

A graph neural network trained on field-solver results turns interconnect geometry directly into RLC values. Many routing, pitch and stack variants can be compared in seconds, with the field solvers available to check the candidates that matter.

03 / Circuit simulation

Fast circuit simulation for eye analysis

Transistor-level drivers and receivers are simulated with the extracted interconnect over long pseudo-random bit streams. Process corners, temperature and data rate are swept in one run.

04 / Channel analysis

Channel simulation and eye diagrams

Eye height, eye width and jitter at every receiver along a net, including nets that branch to more than one die. Rate sweeps show where each eye starts to close.

05 / BER

Bit-error-rate analysis

Statistical eyes and BER contours from the simulated channel, so margins can be read at the error rate the link has to meet.

06 / Noise sources

Crosstalk, supply noise and jitter together

Neighbouring wires switch as aggressors, the driver and receiver cells see the supply noise of the power delivery network, and random jitter is added at the source. Each source can be switched on alone to see which one limits the margin.
