The gap

Heterogeneous integration broke the sequential design flow

2.5D and 3D chiplet packages couple electrical, thermal, reliability, and mechanical physics into a single design problem. The tools accurate enough to trust are far too slow to keep in the loop — so integrity gets checked last, when it is most expensive to fix.

🕸️

Sign-off doesn't scale

Direct transient solves on million-node grids and full 3D FEM thermal runs cost minutes each. A design-space search needs thousands of evaluations.

🔗

The physics is coupled

IR-drop drives Joule heating; heat drives electromigration and thermomigration; thermal mismatch drives warpage. Analyzed in isolation, each one lies.

🤖

Agents need callable physics

An autonomous design agent can only optimize what it can invoke. GUI-bound, hand-run analysis cannot close the loop.
