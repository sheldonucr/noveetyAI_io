Adaptive physical reasoning

Not just always-on physics. Physics that knows where it matters.

Running physics everywhere, all the time, at one resolution spends most of the compute on regions that cannot fail, and is too slow to explore with. Adaptive physical reasoning puts fidelity where the physics is hard, at the resolution you ask for, so each design question gets an answer accurate enough to decide on, at the compute and memory you choose to spend.

Resolution on demand

Fine where it fails, coarse where it can't

The whole package stays in the model. Fine resolution goes where you need it: hot spots, die corners, the gaps between dies. Everything else stays coarse, so you decide how fine to go on the machine you have.

Full package (left) and its hottest compute die at 20 µm (right, own colour scale). Modeled package, synthetic power maps.

Fidelity on demand

The right model for each question

A GNN physics foundational model screens thousands of candidates in milliseconds. APR then explores the design space at the right resolution, with detail only where the decision depends on it, and full resolution signs off. Each question uses the cheapest model that is accurate enough to decide.

Why NoveetyAI

Built for how chiplets actually fail.

Advanced packages fail where physics couples, and the cheapest time to find out is early, while the design can still change.

01 · Coupled physics

Electrical, thermal and mechanical, coupled

IR-drop heats the die, heat ages the wires, mismatch bends the package. Thermal, warpage, electromigration, power-integrity and signal-integrity engines work together, so real temperature maps drive reliability instead of a uniform-die guess.

02 · Exploration speed

Fast enough to explore, accurate enough to decide

AI and reduced-order models sweep thousands of candidates, and resolution on demand adds detail only where a decision hinges on it. Fast answers are checked against full-resolution solves, with the accuracy published in our white papers.

03 · Your flow

Plugs into what you run

Start from the files your teams already have: 3Dblox, GDSII, IPC-2581 and ODB++ in, structured results out. Works with OpenROAD, commercial EDA flows and agentic platforms such as Ergodex AI. Explore early, then sign off at high resolution here or in the flow you already use.

The engines

A complete multiphysics portfolio for 3D IC design exploration and sign-off

Thermal, warpage, electromigration, power integrity and signal integrity. Every engine pairs fast AI and reduced-order models with a full-resolution solver, and every analysis is a CLI call ChipletStack AI Agent can make inside an optimization loop.
