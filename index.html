Adaptive physical reasoning

Not just always-on physics. Physics that knows where it matters.

Running physics everywhere, all the time, at one resolution still spends most of the compute on regions that cannot fail. Adaptive physical reasoning puts fidelity where the physics is hard, at the resolution you ask for, so one analysis is both high fidelity and computationally efficient.

Resolution on demand

Fine where it fails, coarse where it can't

The whole package stays in the model. Fine resolution goes where you need it: hot spots, die corners, the gaps between dies. Everything else stays coarse.

Full package (left) and its hottest compute die at 20 µm (right, own colour scale). Modeled package, synthetic power maps.

Fidelity on demand

The right model for each question

AI surrogates explore, reduced-order solvers refine, and the full numerical reference signs off. Each question uses the cheapest model that is still right.

Why NoveetyAI

Built for how chiplets actually fail.

Advanced packages fail where physics couples, and margins are only as good as the reference behind them.

01 · Coupled physics

Electrical, thermal and mechanical, coupled

IR-drop heats the die, heat ages the wires, mismatch bends the package. Thermal, warpage, electromigration and power-integrity engines work together, so real temperature maps drive reliability instead of a uniform-die guess.

02 · Verified sign-off

Every fast answer, checked

Each engine carries its own full numerical reference. Fast answers are confirmed against it in the same tool, and the accuracy is published in our white papers and benchmarks.

03 · Your flow

Plugs into what you run

Start from the files your teams already have: 3Dblox, GDSII, IPC-2581 and ODB++ in, structured results out. Works with OpenROAD, commercial EDA flows and agentic platforms such as Ergodex AI.

The engines

Four engines enforce and gate the physics — AI accelerates them

Every engine pairs fast AI and reduced-order models with a full numerical reference, and every analysis is a CLI call an agent can make. Our main focus today is thermal and warpage: ThermStack and WarpStack.
