Thermal analysis · ThermStack

ThermStack: Geometry-Resolved Static and Transient Thermal Analysis for 2.5D/3D Chiplet Stacks

A resolution-decoupled numerical solver, a bundled FEM-3D reference and a graph-neural surrogate. 0.509 K from 3D FEM at up to 182× the speed across eight IEEE 3Dblox packages; 0.32 K at 10.6 ms per map for design-space sweeps.

Thermal-mechanical stress · WarpStack

WarpStack: Warpage Sign-off and Design-Space Exploration for 2.5D/3D Chiplet Packages

Detailed 3D analysis, a physics-based MOR-model and a graph-neural predictor from one design description. On a 2nm CoWoS package: within 0.036 µm of 3D at 798,000× the speed, and full maps in 19.4 ms.
